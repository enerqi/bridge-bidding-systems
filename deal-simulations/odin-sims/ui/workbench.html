scenarios
generate deals
Deals thousands of random hands, keeps only the ones that fit the scenario you picked — "North opens 1C", say — and writes the survivors to a page you can click through, one deal at a time. 48 is a normal practice set. The page draws each hand as cards and, on the html-cards format, adds the trick table and the card-combination advice for the play.
idle
analyse one deal
Takes one deal you already have — from a club result, a screenshot, a hand you misplayed — and reports how the play goes. Give it declarer and dummy only (write both defenders as a single -) and it works out every way the defenders' cards can lie. Give it all four hands and it solves the deal exactly. With sample above 0 it also deals the unknown cards out hundreds of times and solves each one, which is the honest "how often does this contract make". Or drop a file on this window: a screenshot of a hand diagram is read by hand-ocr and analysed with the settings below (the deal it read lands in the box, so a misread card can be corrected and re-analysed); a .pbn or .lin is analysed straight; an .html page is opened.
